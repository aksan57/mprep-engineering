DESIGN APPROACH

Understand the process. Design the equipment. Evaluate the engineering.

Equipment design begins with understanding the process, the material being handled, and the required operating conditions.

I approach each design by translating these requirements into equipment geometry, identifying relevant thermal and structural considerations, and evaluating the proposed concept through engineering calculations and simulation where appropriate.

The objective is to develop technically documented equipment concepts that can support further engineering development, design review, and potential fabrication.
